ai2svelte `v1.1.0` adds a built-in preview server. It renders your exported component in a real Svelte app, so you can check how it looks and behaves without setting up a separate project.

The preview refreshes automatically when you change your ai2svelte settings or styles. You can also click `Refresh Preview` to refresh it yourself.

Use the preview to try out settings and styles before you export to your project. To go back to your last saved settings and styles, right-click the panel and choose `Reload`.

:::note
Preview hasn't been tested on Windows yet. Contributions to improve Windows support are welcome.
:::
